Presented at the **2025 Korean Society of Zoonoses (KSZ) Conference** held at Seoul National University: "Spatial Spread Prediction of Emerging Infectious Diseases using On-Device Agentic AI Framework."

**🎉 Congratulations to student Hyungseo Jeon for the successful presentation!**

In this study, we analyzed the global spread of **Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD)** (2019-2025) to validate the effectiveness of the proposed framework (Poster Session 22).

### **Key Research Highlights**

- **Predicting Non-linear 'Spatial Jumps':** Successfully predicted "Spatial Jumps"—non-linear diffusion events that traditional epidemiological models struggle to capture—using **Agentic AI**.
- **High Accuracy & XAI:** Proposed a new AI framework offering superior accuracy and **Explainable AI (XAI)** capabilities compared to traditional Machine Learning methods.
- **On-Device Security:** All inference processes are handled **On-Device**, providing a powerful and practical solution for data privacy and security issues inherent in sensitive infectious disease data.
