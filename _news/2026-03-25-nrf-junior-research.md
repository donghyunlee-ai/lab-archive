Our lab has been selected for the **National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) Outstanding Young Scientist Grant**! 🎊

**Project Title**: *Development of Physics-AI Integrated Dynamic Graph Meta-Learning Based Algal Bloom Prediction Technology for Overcoming Data Scarcity*

**Project Period**: 2026.03.01 – 2029.02.28 (3 years)

This project aims to develop a physics-AI integrated dynamic graph meta-learning framework for accurate and reliable algal bloom prediction under data-scarce environments. By combining physics-based models with advanced AI techniques, we seek to overcome the limitations of sparse observational data and enable robust forecasting of harmful algal blooms.

We look forward to producing meaningful research outcomes over the next three years. Stay tuned!
