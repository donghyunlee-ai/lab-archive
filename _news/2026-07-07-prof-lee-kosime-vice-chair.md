We are pleased to announce that **Professor Donghyun Lee** has been appointed as **Vice Chair of the Special Committee on Energy Industry and Technology Policy** of the **Korea Society for Innovation Management & Economics (KOSIME)**.

Founded in 1992, KOSIME is a leading academic society in Korea dedicated to interdisciplinary research on technological innovation, technology management, and science and technology policy. In this role, Professor Lee will contribute to shaping research and policy discussions on energy industry and technology policy. Congratulations! 🎉
