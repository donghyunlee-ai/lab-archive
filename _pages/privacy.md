  <p>

    you, such as your name or email address. Advertising features, ad
    personalization, and Google signals are not used.

  </p>
  <p>

    site in your browser settings, use your browser's tracking-protection or
    private-browsing mode, or install the
    <a href="https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout" rel="noopener" target="_blank">
    Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on</a>. Opting out does not limit your
    use of this site in any way.
  </p>
