Effective date: August 24, 2026

This website is operated by the Donghyun Lee AI Group, Division of Social Science & AI, Hankuk University of Foreign Studies. This notice explains what information is processed when you visit this site.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used and to improve its content. Google Analytics collects information such as a randomly generated client identifier stored in first-party cookies (_ga, _ga_*), pages visited, visit times, referral source, browser, operating system, device type, and approximate region. We do not send Google Analytics any information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address. Advertising features, ad personalization, and Google signals are not used.

Analytics data is processed by Google LLC (USA). User- and event-level data is retained for up to 14 months; aggregated statistics that are not linked to an individual identifier may be kept longer. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

To opt out of Google Analytics, you can block or delete cookies for this site in your browser settings, use your browser's tracking-protection or private-browsing mode, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Opting out does not limit your use of this site in any way.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may log technical information such as visitor IP addresses for security and operational purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Email contact

If you contact us by email, we use the information you provide (your address, name, affiliation, and message) only to review and respond to your inquiry. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message.
